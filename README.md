This project focuses on analysing Zepto's business and sales performance using data analytics techniques. The objective is to identify sales trends, profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, payment preferences and delivery efficiency. The project uses a dataset containing 5,015 records and 25 columns covering sales transactions, products, customers, locations, discounts, estimated costs, profits and delivery operations for 2025.
**Key Business Insights**
UPI is the most popular payment method, accounting for approximately 51.8% of transaction records.
Delhi generates the highest order value among the 12 cities.
Baby Care is the highest-performing category by order value.
Approximately 93.9% of records are marked as delivered.
The estimated overall profit margin is approximately 23.34%.
The on-time delivery rate is approximately 63.9% among records with delivery classification.
